# Privacy Policy — My Inventory Refresher

**Last updated: November 21, 2026**

## Overview

My Inventory Refresher is a privately developed application designed to manage and refresh eBay inventory listings.

The application is intended for use by its owner and is not offered as a public service.

## Information Accessed

With the account holder's authorization, the application may access:

- eBay listing information, including titles, descriptions, prices, photographs, and item specifics.
- Inventory quantities and listing statuses.
- Seller account information required for authorized listing management.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used to review existing listings, research product details, prepare listing updates, and perform authorized inventory management operations.

The application may use external product-information services to obtain pricing and product metadata.

## Data Storage

Application settings, listing records, and preparation history may be stored locally on the owner's computer.

Authentication credentials and tokens should be stored securely and must not be publicly disclosed.

## Information Sharing

The application is not intended to sell personal information or provide customer data to advertisers.

Information may be exchanged with eBay and configured product-research services as necessary for the application's functionality.

## Account Authorization

Access to eBay information requires authorization from the account holder.

The account holder may revoke application authorization through their eBay account settings.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as application functionality changes.

## Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, contact the application owner through the contact information provided with the application.
